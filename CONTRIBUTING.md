# Contributing

Suggestions, corrections, and new book entries are welcome.

## For a new entry

Please provide:

- Full title and original-language title where available
- Author(s), editor(s), and translator(s), with roles identified
- Country or strategic tradition of origin, and subject country if different
- Original publication year and the edition being cited
- Publisher or issuing institution
- ISBN for the cited edition, or “not identified” if it cannot be verified
- A concise summary written in your own words
- At least one reliable source, preferably a publisher, national library, university library, or official institution
- Work type: primary text, doctrine / professional military education, or scholarly analysis

## Editorial guidelines

- Use neutral, descriptive language. Do not present an author's argument as a country's official policy unless an authoritative source establishes that status.
- Distinguish the book's origin from the location of its publisher and from the country it analyzes.
- Identify the language and translation when relevant. ISBNs are edition-specific.
- Mark uncertain details rather than guessing.
- Use stable bibliographic links where possible.
- Keep summaries brief and original. Avoid copying publisher descriptions or substantial passages.
- Do not upload scans, full texts, cover images, or substantial excerpts without clear rights to redistribute them.
- Inclusion is not an endorsement, ranking, or purchase recommendation.

For a correction, open an issue or submit a pull request with the source supporting the change.
